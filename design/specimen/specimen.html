3 · Voice and tap

Core product interaction.

The voice is on this word, the reader tapped that one, and this one is both.
  • wash.fill plus wash.ink · the spoken word.
  • mark.selected · the tapped word.
  • mark.coincide · ring when the tap lands on the spoken word.
Audio paused: the wash freezes here.
  • rule.paused plus rule.ring · the frozen wash.
