The quiet reader

A reader listens to a long article about careful listening while the paper moves slowly through quiet rooms. Every word waits for its turn in the order that the author chose, with patience and small details that matter more than grand claims.

The article out-argues its own summary. It does not hurry. A sentence opens, turns once, and closes on a smaller word than it began with. The reader follows the wash and lets the voice carry the line.

Some paragraphs are short. Others run longer, with commas, abbreviations such as Dr. and U.S., decimals such as 3.5, and a pause that asks the reader to wait. The point is not speed. The point is that the next word arrives before the reader wonders where the voice went.

When the reader pauses, the room holds still. When the reader plays again, the voice resumes at the same word. A scrub moves the highlight and the voice follows on release. A lookup opens a card, and the card closes back to the place it came from.

This text exists only for the benchmark. It is long enough to open the reader, short enough to run many times, and plain enough that every run measures the same plan.
